Dragon Quest IX Modern v47: RecompLauncher support, trophies in the Esc menu, and fixes for the stick, the controls and the map.

**What's new since v46**
- **RecompLauncher**: the game can now be installed and updated from RecompLauncher. Each release gets a `Dragon-Quest-IX-Modern-vN-Windows-x64.zip` file (sources only, no game data), and the launcher builds the game from your own ROM. A zipped ROM (.zip holding the .nds) is accepted too.
- **Trophies in the Esc menu**: the trophy list now opens from the in-game menu (Esc > Trophies), right under Resume, instead of a Journal tab.
- **Quests of caves and huts on the map**: small places drawn on their field's map (the Spirit Pool near Angel Falls, the caves around Stornway...) now show their quests and a badge on their door.

**Fixed since v46**
- The hero no longer stays frozen after a battle when you hold the stick while it ends.
- Game > Controls opened from the Esc menu: Change key and Controller button work again. Controller button now waits for a controller button only (a key pressed meanwhile no longer takes its place), and says "No controller connected" when none is.
- Gathering stars on the map follow the real sparkles: no more gold star where nothing can be picked up. A spot that comes back while you are in the field shows a grey star until you enter the area again.

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`, then choose your own ROM (USA or European). Or install it from RecompLauncher. **Already on v31.x or later?** The game offers the update when it starts (or Help > Check for updates): your saves and settings are kept.

**Not included, on purpose**: no ROM, no game executable and no data from the game. Everything is built on your PC from the ROM you own.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you update to v47.
