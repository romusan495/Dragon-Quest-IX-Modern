Dragon Quest IX Modern v45: gathering spots on the map, and fixes for the new Equip screen and the Records' books.

**What's new since v44**
- **Gathering spots on the map**: each field's gathering spot (alchemy ingredients) gets a star, gold where something sparkles now, grey while it's empty (with "also mark the emptied ones"). Hover it with the mouse to see the item. On by default, in the legend, and can be turned off in the Modern menu.
- **Equip screen with a controller**: the right trigger shows the next character and the left trigger the one before (LT/RT signs beside the name), unless you gave a trigger to a DS button in Game > Controls.

**Fixed since v44**
- Equip screen: switching to another character no longer drops back to the DS layout, the item's description and stats are no longer cut away with HD text on, and the job plaque and coat of arms show without the HD texture pack.
- Records' books (Wardrobe, Item list, Bestiary, Alchimanach, titles): with HD text on, the right column of names is shown again (only 8 names were shown before).
- Monsters walking on the map: no more blurry smudges in the wrong colours with the HD texture pack (they are enlarged with xBR instead). Packs already made are fixed without remaking them.
- Minimap: the room plans now show in the inn (Quester's Rest) and its VIP rooms.
- Inviting a companion at Patty's: their stats show in a proper window again, not as letters floating over the list.
- Quest list: no more brown streak under the last quest.

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`, then choose your own ROM (USA or European). **Already on v31.x or later?** The game offers the update when it starts (or Help > Check for updates): your saves and settings are kept.

**Not included, on purpose**: no ROM, no game executable and no data from the game. Everything is built on your PC from the ROM you own.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you update to v45.
