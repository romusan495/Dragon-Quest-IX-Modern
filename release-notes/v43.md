Dragon Quest IX Modern v43: better indoor minimaps, and the two-screen movies in one picture.

**What's new since v42**
- **Indoor minimaps**: wardrobes and drawers now get a marker like chests, and they count on the buildings' badges and in the legend. Story bookshelves are a brighter green and recipe bookshelves a brighter violet.
- **Two-screen movies in widescreen**: the opening movie and "To be continued" are made of two halves played together. They now show as one tall picture, the top half over the bottom one, with the same picture blurred on the window's sides, instead of only the bottom half with the top one hidden under the minimap.

**Fixed since v42**
- Indoor minimaps: in most houses and castles the hero and the chests were drawn squeezed toward the middle of the room's plan. They are now at their real place.
- Indoor minimaps: a chest, pot or barrel standing by a wall no longer sits outside the room's plan.
- Quest list: in widescreen, black stains no longer pop up over the menu (the notice board's ink).
- Desktop shortcut: its icon no longer turns into a black square after an update.

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`, then choose your own ROM (USA or European). **Already on v31.x or later?** The game offers the update when it starts (or Help > Check for updates): your saves and settings are kept.

**Not included, on purpose**: no ROM, no game executable and no data from the game. Everything is built on your PC from the ROM you own.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you update to v43.
