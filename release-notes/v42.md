Dragon Quest IX Modern v42: smoother frame rate on laptops, and fixes for brief flashes on screen.

**What's new since v41**
- **Laptops use their dedicated graphics card**: on PCs with two graphics chips (Intel plus NVIDIA or AMD), the game now starts on the NVIDIA/AMD card instead of the slower built-in one. This fixes frame rate drops in places like the Observatory.
- **3D resolution Auto adapts to your graphics card**: when the card is too busy, Auto lowers the 3D resolution a little to keep 60 FPS, and raises it again once things are quiet. A fixed resolution (2x, 4x…) is left as you chose it.
- **Battle text box in the Modern window style**: the message box at the bottom of battles now has square corners and the thin grey line, like the experience window (thanks Tsu).

**Fixed since v41**
- HP, MP and level no longer flash at the top of the 3D view when the field menu opens or closes.
- No more one-frame flashes when the 3D resolution changes on its own (Auto): a pink frame during a fade to black, or the two screens flashing for a moment in a menu (thanks Tsu).

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`, then choose your own ROM (USA or European). **Already on v31.x or later?** The game offers the update when it starts (or Help > Check for updates): your saves and settings are kept.

**Not included, on purpose**: no ROM, no game executable and no data from the game. Everything is built on your PC from the ROM you own.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you update to v42.
