Dragon Quest IX Modern v2.0: townspeople in 3D, a modern title screen and character creation (thanks Tsu).

**New version numbers**: after v47 comes v2.0. From now on, fixes are v2.1, v2.2... and big additions v3, v4... If you are on v47 or older, the game's update message calls this one "v48 (v2.0)".

**What's new since v47**
- **Townspeople in 3D** (Graphics > Townspeople in 3D, on by default): townspeople can be drawn as real 3D characters instead of flat pictures. A few are made from Dragon Quest IX itself. For many more, put your own Dragon Quest VII and/or Dragon Quest VIII (Nintendo 3DS) in the new `rom\3DS` folder and run BUILD.bat again (see `rom\3DS\README.txt`). Nothing from those games is included.
- **Modern title screen**: the opening movie plays behind "Start your adventure", and the adventure log menu sits small at the bottom right so the logo stays in view.
- **Character creation redone for wide screens** (thanks Tsu): the steps and your choices on the left, the character big in the middle on a gold ring, the choices on the right, and your own keys shown with the game's words. Patty's Invite a friend shows the list and the companion's stats side by side.
- **New Priest and Martial Artist insignias** (thanks Tsu): an ornate cross and a fist with rays, on the plaques and the coats of arms.

**Fixed since v47** (thanks Tsu)
- X menu with HD text: the highlight band covers its whole column again.
- Skill points: the "Cancel the allocation?" prompt no longer leaves pieces of the X menu around it.
- Opening movie: no more pieces of a Records page over it.
- Equip card: the HD description stops before the dotted edge, and the equipped mark shows with HD text.

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`, then choose your own ROM (USA or European). Or install it from RecompLauncher. **Already on v31.x or later?** The game offers the update when it starts (or Help > Check for updates): your saves and settings are kept.

**Not included, on purpose**: no ROM, no game executable and no data from any game. Everything is built on your PC from the ROMs you own.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you update to v2.0.
