Dragon Quest IX Modern v44: a modern Equip screen, the Records' books redone, and new job plaques (thanks Tsu).

**What's new since v43** (with the Modern window style)
- **Equip screen redone**: the character stands whole in the middle of a weapon shop, with their name, job and level above (L/R to switch), the item's card on the left and the gear list on the right. The shown job's coat of arms hangs on the shop's wall. The mouse works on the list's rows, and the bag has a clean modern grid with each item in its own colours.
- **Records' books in one modern window each**: the Bestiary, Alchimanach, Accolades, Wardrobe, Item list and quest list now show the list on the left and the card (or quest sheet) on the right, instead of over it. In the Bestiary, the keyboard goes up and down the whole page, the mouse works, and each family has its logo.
- **New job plaques**: each job's symbol in polished metal on a dark plate, in battle, in the menus' name bar and on item cards. On an item card, the "Equips:" line shows the jobs' plaques, faint for those who can't equip it.
- **Item cards, shops and Yes/No windows** in the modern style: a cleaner item card, the shop's description window as thin as its list, and two Yes/No windows close together no longer merge into one.

**Fixed since v43**
- Walking with the stick right after a menu or a dialogue: the hero no longer walks off the wrong way or stands still.
- Map monsters: in fields away from Stornway (Angel Falls' region, for example), the monsters' red dots are now around the hero, not off to one side.

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`, then choose your own ROM (USA or European). **Already on v31.x or later?** The game offers the update when it starts (or Help > Check for updates): your saves and settings are kept.

**Not included, on purpose**: no ROM, no game executable and no data from the game. Everything is built on your PC from the ROM you own.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you update to v44.
