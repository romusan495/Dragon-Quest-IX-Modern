Dragon Quest IX Modern v46: trophies like on Steam, a round minimap and modern menus (thanks Tsu).

**What's new since v45**
- **Trophies**: 144 trophies to unlock as you play (story, quest and grotto bosses, legacy bosses, metal monsters, Records collections, quests, skills, vocation levels, battles). Each one has a bronze, silver or gold grade, and a platinum trophy rewards all the others. A notice pops up in the corner with a short chime when you earn one.
- **Trophies tab in the Journal**: ten categories in the order of the adventure, your medal count per grade, and the locked trophies sorted by grade. Boss and quest trophies stay hidden until you earn them, and you can reveal one with A if you want.
- **Trophy pictures**: the first build after the update downloads each trophy's badge picture from RetroAchievements onto your PC (a progress bar shows it). Without Internet, the game simply shows cups instead, and the build goes on.
- Your unlocks are kept in `trophies.txt`. In `settings.ini`, `trophies = 0` turns trophies off and `trophy_sound = 0` mutes the chime.
- **Round minimap** (on by default, Modern > Map in the corner: round): the map around the hero in a circle with a silver compass ring and the place's name under it. Nearby monsters get one colour per kind, with their names listed beside the map.
- **Modern X menu**: each menu window alone in the middle with its path, the chosen line lit, smooth transitions, and no more flashes of the old layout when a message opens or an item is used. The bag shows one list in the middle, each item's icon before its name and its description under the list. Equip compares the stats.
- **Attributes and Equip**: Attributes shows the character's 3D model alone, their own equipped items' icons and their job's plaque. Equip shows which key changes character.
- **Battle records (Sandy)**: her page, the title ceremony, and the Wardrobe, Item list and quests menus are redrawn in modern windows. The quest list is laid out for a wide screen: the list on its own, the quest beside it with its big sign, the Complete! stamp and the date.

**Fixed since v45**
- Monster list in the battle records: each monster's page shows again.
- Empty lists show "This list is empty." whole, with no blank blocks when leaving a list.
- The title screen is shown as the game draws it again, without a dark veil.

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`, then choose your own ROM (USA or European). **Already on v31.x or later?** The game offers the update when it starts (or Help > Check for updates): your saves and settings are kept.

**Not included, on purpose**: no ROM, no game executable and no data from the game. Everything is built on your PC from the ROM you own.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you update to v46.
